#### The Perry’s experience at Schaumburg
Located near the Hyatt Regency, Perry’s is proud to serve our second Chicagoland location in Schaumburg, Illinois! Perry’s Schaumburg brings our award-winning menu, amazing ambiance, unique signatures, tableside presentations and our exceptional service to one exquisite location. This innovative, 12,000-square-foot restaurant boasts a vibrant color palette with details of modern chandeliers and textures that embellish our restaurant and celebrate the local community. The main dining room, featuring a spectacular wine wall, allows guests to catch glimpses of chefs creating innovations in the kitchen. Our four beautifully appointed private dining rooms are the perfect place to make your intimate milestones cherished memories. Bar 79®, which opens to an inviting, heated patio, is the ideal spot to sip and savor refreshing, creative cocktails. Perry’s Schaumburg undoubtedly brings the best of the Rare and Well Done® experience to more of Chicago.

Perry’s portfolio includes our Premier Cru Champagne, Chardonnay, Rosé, Pinot Noir, Big Red Blend, and Cabernet Sauvignon, available by the glass, by the bottle and select varietals in larger formats.
**Perry’s Reserve Wines are now available for shipping directly to your door!** At long last, the wines you love from our tables are now yours to uncork at home. Whether you’re toasting the holidays, gifting a client, or savoring a special evening, you can now curate your perfect collection from Perry’s Reserve Wines.
[Shop Now](https://shopperryswines.com)
NEW **Perry’s Reserve Premier Cru Champagne** is a grower’s cuvée from the revered Chigny-les-Roses village near Reims, France. This emblematic Carte d’Or delivers a refined golden color with fine bubbles, delicate white flowers, and delightful citrus freshness on the palate.
**Perry’s Reserve Chardonnay** , born from grapes slowly ripened to perfection in select Sonoma County vineyards, offers enticing aromas of ripe pear, apricot, orange zest and lush tropical fruits. This pour is specially blended to pair perfectly with our seafood and poultry options, such as our crab cakes and lobster.
**Perry’s Reserve Rosé** uses the highest quality 100% Pinot Noir grapes sourced from cool coastal Monterey County vineyards, delivering inviting aromas and flavors of strawberry, blood orange, and cranberry. These bright, crisp fruit flavors are complemented by a soft, elegant texture on the palate and a refreshing finish. This wine deliciously complements Perry’s Signature Fried Asparagus, Seafood Stuffed Mushrooms and Chicken Oscar.
**Perry’s Reserve Pinot Noir** comes from cool coastal Monterey vineyards to offer inviting aromas of bright red fruits of cherry, strawberry, and raspberry. Sip its savory, fruit-forward notes alongside the sweet, smoky sizzle of our Famous Pork Chop for the perfect pairing and truly uncorked experience.
**Perry’s Reserve Big Red Blend** is carefully crafted from extraordinary vineyard sites across Napa Valley and offers aromas of red and dark fruits, cracked pepper, tobacco leaf and spices. The palate shows bright acidity and integrated tannins provide a lingering, bright finish to pair beautifully with our prime ribeye.
**Perry’s Reserve Cabernet Sauvignon** is highly intriguing with its complexity from Sonoma soils and offers rich aromas of black currants, blackberries, anise, cocoa, and cigar box. With a smooth, lingering finish, the wine pairs exceptionally well with filet mignon.
